ABOUT M.D. MEMORIAL

Learning with Purpose, Growing with Confidence

A Strong Foundation for Every Learner

M.D. Memorial School, located in Aurangabad, Bulandshahr, is committed to helping children develop academic skills, confidence, creativity and respect for others.

Our school encourages curiosity, communication, teamwork and responsible behaviour alongside classroom learning.
